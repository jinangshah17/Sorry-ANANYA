Dear Ananya 💜,

Agar tum yeh letter padh rahi ho, toh sabse pehle thank you. Thank you for itna time nikalne ke liye aur is chhote se museum tak aane ke liye.

Sach bolu toh mujhe perfect words nahi aate. Main speeches dene wala insaan bhi nahi hu. Lekin mujhe itna zaroor pata hai ki hamari friendship mere liye important hai. Bahut zyada.

Pichhle kuch dino mein jo bhi hua, usne mujhe ek cheez realise karwayi: kabhi kabhi hum gusse mein ya frustration mein aisi cheezein bol dete hain jo hum actually feel nahi karte. Aur shayad maine bhi kuch aisa hi kiya.

Main yeh museum isliye nahi bana raha ki main sahi prove ho jaun. Main yeh museum isliye bana raha hu kyunki mujhe lagta hai ki hamari friendship ek argument se zyada valuable hai.

Jab bhi main hamari chats, bakchodi, random jokes, stupid conversations aur woh moments yaad karta hu jahan hum bina wajah haste rehte the... tab lagta hai ki yeh sab lose karna worth hi nahi hai.

Tum meri life ka woh chapter ho jo boring days ko interesting bana deta hai. Aur honestly, mujhe woh chapter band nahi karna.

Main yeh promise nahi kar sakta ki future mein kabhi galti nahi hogi. Main human hu. Tum bhi ho. Kabhi na kabhi hum phir disagree karenge. Lekin main yeh promise kar sakta hu ki friendship ko ego se upar rakhne ki koshish hamesha karunga.

Aur agar tum mujhe ek aur chance dena chaho, toh main genuinely us friendship ko appreciate karunga.

Agar abhi bhi gussa ho... toh thoda aur reh sakti ho 😭. Lekin please permanently mat rehna.

Because life already kaafi serious hai, aur mujhe meri favourite idiot best friend wapas chahiye.

With respect, regret, and a lot of friendship 💜
— Jinang
